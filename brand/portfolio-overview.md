# Offline Scout

**Scouting that works with no signal, free for every team.**

At FRC provincials, our scouting form wouldn't load in a gym with no
service. So we built an app that never needs it.

- **For every team:** free, open source, FTC and FRC.
- **Mental health:** short scouting shifts and a calm, simple screen.
- **Sustainability:** no paper sheets; one app reused every season.
