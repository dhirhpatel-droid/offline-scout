# Offline Scout

Offline Scout is a free scouting app for FTC and FRC teams that works with
no internet at all. A Lead picks the event, and the schedule loads straight
from FIRST. The Lead then assigns who watches which robot. Scouts tap through
each match on their own phones, and every tap is saved on the phone, so
nothing is lost when the gym has no signal. Matches send to the team once
there's signal again, or phone to phone by QR code. The Lead then sees every
team's averages and gets a warning when two scouts disagree, so alliance
picks rest on data the team can trust.
