# Offline Scout

**Scouting that never needs signal.**

Offline Scout is a free scouting app built for the stands, where the wifi
dies first. Every tap saves straight to the scout's phone, so a match is
never lost, even with zero service. Before the event, Leads load the official
FIRST schedule and give each scout their robots. During matches, scouts tap
big, simple buttons with their eyes on the field. When signal returns, or by
a quick QR scan, everything comes together into one picture of every team,
and the app flags any match where two scouts disagree. The result is data a
team can trust when it counts most: alliance selection.

Free for every FTC and FRC team · offline-scout.vercel.app
