# Offline Scout

**Scouting that works with no signal, built for every team.**

## Why we built it
At FRC provincials, the gym had no service. Our online scouting form wouldn't
even load, and we lost our scouting data right when alliance selection
depended on it. Every team in those stands had the same problem. So we
built Offline Scout: a free scouting app that **works with zero internet,
always**. Every tap is saved on the phone, and matches send to the team
whenever signal comes back, or phone-to-phone by QR code with no internet
at all.

## Built for other teams, not just us
- **Free and open source,** so any FTC or FRC team can use it from the
  first event, with no budget and no data plan.
- **Works for FTC and FRC.** A new season's game is one config file, not a
  rewrite, so the app keeps working year after year.
- **Installs from a link.** No app store, on the phones students already have.

## Our initiatives
- **Mental health:** scouting all day is draining. Leads plan short
  shifts, so no one is stuck in the stands for every match, and Coverage
  shows the gaps fairly. Big buttons and one match period on screen at a
  time keep it calm, and nothing is ever lost, so there's no panic when the
  wifi dies.
- **Sustainability:** no paper scouting sheets to print and throw away, and
  no new hardware. The app is built to last: each season is reused through
  a config file instead of being rebuilt.

## What it does
Leads pick the event, and its schedule loads from FIRST. They assign who
watches which robot. Scouts tap through each match in seconds, and Leads
get every team's averages, with **Data checks** that flag when two scouts
disagree, so alliance picks rest on numbers the team can trust.
