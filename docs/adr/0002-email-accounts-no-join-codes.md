# Email accounts and email invites, no join codes

Every Scout signs up with email and password, and joins a Scouting group only through a Lead's email Invite. Join codes were considered (faster to share in person) but rejected for now because anyone who sees a code can join and read a group's data. Accounts tie every Submission to a person, which the Lead views, Assignments and Data checks depend on.

Because most users are under 18, we store only an email and a display name, and the app explains what is stored and why.
